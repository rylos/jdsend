# jdsend — Privacy Policy

jdsend has no server of its own and collects nothing. It does not track you, does not
profile you, and sends no analytics anywhere.

## What leaves your browser, and where it goes

One place only: `https://api.jdownloader.org`, the My.JDownloader relay operated by
AppWork GmbH, the makers of JDownloader. It carries the links you choose to send and the
options you set for them — package name, download folder, priority, passwords, comment —
end-to-end encrypted with keys only your own JDownloader shares. Your use of that relay is
governed by [AppWork's own privacy policy](https://my.jdownloader.org/privacy.html).

Nothing is sent until you ask for it: a context menu entry, the toolbar button, the
keyboard shortcut or the form's *Send*.

## Your My.JDownloader account

Signing in derives two keys from your e-mail and password. The keys are kept in the
browser's extension storage; the password itself is discarded and never stored. The keys
never leave your device except as the protocol's signatures, which is how My.JDownloader
knows the request is yours. *Sign out* removes them and ends the session on the server.

Those keys are worth as much as the password, as they are in JDownloader itself: anyone
with access to your browser profile can use them to reach your JDownloader.

## Your browsing

jdsend runs no code on the pages you visit and has no permission to. It reads a page's
address only when you ask it to send that page, and keeps no history of what was sent.

## What it never does

No analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no update checks, no advertising, no
third-party services beyond the My.JDownloader relay above.
